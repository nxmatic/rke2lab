= Manifests Contribution Channels
:toc: left
:toclevels: 3

How a domain hands work to the manifests synthesis. The synthesis does not own
what it assembles: domains *contribute* manifest units, and the synthesis
aggregates them into the Kubernetes payload delivered to the node's
`server/manifests` tree.

[NOTE]
====
*Historical note.* This document once described *three* contribution channels
(manifest units, node-env sections, host-assets). Two of them — the node-env
`KEY=VALUE` env-var channel and the `/srv/host` host-asset materialisation — were
*dissolved* when the node substrate moved to a declarative NixOS base: the node's
identity, PKI, containerd/NRI config, dbus and toolbox are now baked by the
`nixos/` modules, not delivered as synthesised env files or `/srv/host` scripts.
See link:../nixos-substrate/substrate-model.adoc[the NixOS node-substrate target
vision]. What remains here is the one surviving channel — manifest units — plus
the read-only node context threaded to them.
====

== The manifest-unit channel

A domain never writes into the synthesis directly. It publishes a
*contribution* and the synthesis *collects* it. The contribution is a set of
CDK8s constructs synthesised to Kubernetes YAML.

* the contributor is an SCR `@Component(service = ManifestsDomainRegistrar.class)`
  keyed by its manifest `domainId` (`networking`, `storage`, `high-availability`, …);
* the run-scoped policy arrives as a *method parameter* at collection time —
  `ManifestsDomainRegistrar.domain(ManifestDomainPolicy)` — never as construction
  state, so a registrar stays a stateless singleton;
* the aggregator, `DefaultManifestSynthesisService`, collects every registrar with
  one `@Reference(MULTIPLE)` and builds the `ManifestsDomainRegistry` from the
  results — no static registration.

=== Migration to the SCR shape (DONE)

This SCR-discovered wiring is the *completed* migration of the manifest-unit
channel. It was formerly the laggard — statically wired through a hand-maintained
`register(new XxxRegistrar())` chain in `DefaultManifestSynthesisService`, a second
source of truth beside the components. Promoting each registrar to an SCR
`@Component(service = ManifestsDomainRegistrar.class)` and collecting them with a
single `@Reference(MULTIPLE)` erased that static chain — aligning the units channel
onto the SCR contribution *shape* that the node-env and host-asset channels had
pioneered before they were dissolved into the NixOS node substrate. The shape
outlived the two channels that first carried it.

=== Anti-patterns

* *Run state in the constructor* (❌): a registrar built with policy baked in
  cannot be a stateless SCR singleton. ✅ Take the run-scoped policy as a method
  parameter at collection time.
* *A second source of truth* (❌): a hand-maintained `register(new XxxRegistrar())`
  list beside the components. ✅ Publish an SCR `@Component`; let the aggregator
  discover it.

== The node context (read-only, not a contribution)

Units that need the run's cluster/node facts read them from a `NodeEnvContext`
threaded into their `ManifestsUnitContext`. `DefaultNodeEnvContext` derives it once
from the handed-over identity via the netplan blueprint and exposes two narrow
views — `bootstrapIdentity()` and `networkTopology()`. This is a *context*, not a
contribution channel: units consume it, they do not publish into it.
